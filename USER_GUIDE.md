# BreachMirror AI User Guide

## What Is BreachMirror AI?

**BreachMirror AI** is a cybersecurity awareness and incident-response training simulator. It helps users build practical security skills through realistic examples, guided exercises, quizzes, and dashboards.

Users can practice how to:

- Recognize phishing, exposed secrets, unsafe AI use, and device-related risks
- Follow company security policies
- Respond to incidents in a constructive, blame-free way
- Improve security knowledge through scenarios, quizzes, and progress tracking

> **Important:** BreachMirror AI is a training tool. It does not monitor real systems or protect production accounts.

## Available Features

### Login and Registration
Create a registered training account with a Stewart company email, secure password, and assigned role. Registered users can sign in with their username or company email.

### Dashboard Pages

- **Dashboard:** View all enabled training widgets and current progress.
- **Threat Activity:** Review simulated incidents, risk levels, and response guidance.
- **Security Guide:** Read security policies and practical rules for common situations.
- **Analytics:** Explore charts showing simulated threats, attack types, and risk trends.
- **Practice Scenarios:** Work through realistic workplace security decisions and see safer responses.

### Dashboard Tools

- **Security Radar:** Explore simulated threats and attack paths.
- **Private Report:** Test suspicious text, links, code, or secrets and receive guidance.
- **Security Handbook:** Read detailed security policy content and learning sections.
- **Policy Q&A:** Ask a security-policy question and review the answer alongside cited source passages.
- **Code of Conduct:** Review workplace conduct and ethical decision-making guidance.
- **Security Knowledge Check:** Test your understanding of security and conduct policies.
- **AI Practice Tool:** Enter a simulated security action and review its possible risks and safer alternatives.
- **Search:** Find incidents, policies, guidance, and devices.
- **Widget Customizer:** Choose which dashboard widgets appear and arrange them to suit your workflow.

Policy Q&A searches local policy and conduct documents. If Gemini is configured on the server, the question and matching excerpts are sent to Google to draft the answer. Without Gemini, the app shows matching excerpts only.

### Audit Log
The local audit log records registration, login, failed login, lockout, logout, and password-reset events. It is intended for local development and training use.

## Getting Started

1. Register with your Stewart company email.
2. Sign in with your username or company email.
3. Open **Threat Activity** to review a simulated incident.
4. Use the **Security Guide** to learn the recommended response.
5. Try a **Practice Scenario** or **Security Knowledge Check**.
6. Use the dashboard tools to explore, test, and track your learning.
